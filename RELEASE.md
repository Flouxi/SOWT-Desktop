SOWT 0.0.29 desktop preview for Mac (Apple silicon and Intel) and Windows x64.

Connects to https://app.sowt.ai. An internet connection and an existing invited SOWT account are required. No local model, server, Orgo account or provider credentials are bundled.

Preview signing status: unsigned; Mac installers are not Apple-notarized. Operating systems may warn or block installation. You can use https://app.sowt.ai in a browser. Signed installers and automatic desktop updates are not yet configured.

Requirements: macOS 13+, or Windows 10+ x64.

SHA256SUMS.txt lists each installer digest. SOURCE.json records the exact desktop source files copied from the private application repository. Voice availability and allowances follow your SOWT account. Cloud-computer execution is not activated by installing the app.
